## Why the AGPL and not MIT, BSD or Apache?

A permissive license would let anyone, including commercial actors, use,
modify and redistribute the code with no obligation to share their changes
or to support the project. The AGPL's copyleft, including its network-use
clause, is what gives closed-source and commercial users a reason to arrange
a commercial license instead, and keeps that option open as a way to sustain
FST4's development and maintenance at ÚTIA. Everyone else gets the toolbox
for free, with the guarantee that improvements stay open.

## Contributing: the Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

**Every contribution needs a signed CLA before it can be merged.**

Why this matters: you own the copyright to the code you contribute, and
ÚTIA owns only the code it wrote itself. Without a CLA, ÚTIA could not
include your contribution in a commercial (non-AGPL) license offered to
third parties, because that requires *your* permission, not just ÚTIA's,
and the dual-licensing model would break down over time.

The CLA does **not** transfer your copyright away. It grants ÚTIA a broad,
perpetual, irrevocable license to relicense your contribution, including
under commercial or proprietary terms, while you keep your copyright and
remain free to use, relicense or reuse your own contribution elsewhere.

In practice:

- You keep authorship and copyright of your contribution.
- Your contribution remains available to everyone under the AGPL-3.0,
  forever.
- ÚTIA can include your contribution in a commercial license, should one be
  arranged, which is how the project can sustain itself.
- Without a signed CLA, a contribution cannot be merged.

The agreement text is ÚTIA's to issue. It will be published in this
repository, together with the signing procedure, before the first external
contribution is merged; until then, pull requests are welcome for discussion
but cannot be merged. The practical side of contributing (bug reports, pull
requests, the golden gate, the coding rules) is described in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md); security problems go through
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not through public issues.
